# 10 — FUTURE DESIGN (NOT CURRENT WORK)

Keep this material for continuity; **do not implement or hint too obviously in micro-MVP**.

## The eventual larger demo

- Genuine Morrow Creek Mystery Life Sim: more believable daily rhythms, optional relationships/romance, neighborhood history and small personal objectives that are not always paranormal.
- Forest access: a beautiful but increasingly untenable region, spatial ambiguity and very occasional physical danger without immediate monster reveal.
- Incomplete memory / missing residents tied loosely to an ambiguous old presence or continuum. Its motives and ontology are deliberately left open; the player must infer rather than read an explanation.
- Hannah's brother Evan is missing; her parents deny having a son. Historical photo (c. 1998) includes somebody who looks like the protagonist though they cannot remember being there. This is a **mid-story escalation**, not ending of micro-MVP and not necessarily finale of larger demo.
- Do not assume imitation, time travel, doubles, universe copy, rehearsal or ghosts are confirmed. They are interpretations players may form.
- The expression "Stillness Continuum" is **internal lore vocabulary**, not player-facing quest explanation.

## The long-term loop

Sustained town life → friendships, practical obligations, revisits and missed chances → contradictory human testimony / physical evidence / period Web → deeper exploration and consequences. Romance is optional depth, not an independent 50% minigame. One season (golden autumn 2003) avoids huge four-season asset burden and strengthens mood.

## Questions left deliberately unresolved

Why does the protagonist appear in an older photograph? What lies in the forest? Are memories false, inverted, rearranged, or something stranger? Why is the place trapped in autumn? Can anyone leave? We should eventually develop author-side consistency and possible answers, but no rushed canonical explanation should be hardcoded just to justify a micro-MVP scare.
