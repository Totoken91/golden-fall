# 00 — GOLDEN FALL: AUTHORITATIVE CANON AND GUARDRAILS

## North star
*A town you want to spend an evening in. A diner where you might return just to talk. Then an impossible fragment of an ordinary conversation behind the building makes the world unsafe in a way the player cannot categorize.*

**Genre:** first-person Mystery Life Sim with psychological/folk horror; the romance route/stat-manager idea is demoted from primary genre to possible later narrative depth. Not 50/50 dating and horror; one system of people, places, information and time.

**Setting:** fictional Morrow Creek, Oregon, October 2003. Woods, former timber industry, small-town diner, older cars, record/CD shop, rain, grunge and intimate acoustic folk. Something linked to the forest and disappearances affects recollection and possibly reality. Its existence, form, motives, limits and chronology remain ambiguous. The town may sit in a "Stillness Continuum," a continuous golden autumn. Treat this as atmosphere/unconfirmed lore rather than something to exposite in MVP.

**Working title:** `Golden Fall`. Audience and ALL player-facing strings: US English. Title is provisional, not a claim of trademark clearance.

**Look:** Golden Autumn Gothic. Orange leaves, tobacco browns, warm tungsten, burnished metal, gloomy dark evergreens, gray rain. **Not** blue-tinted Twilight, hyperreal concept-art level detail, cute anime or generic procedural AI asset sludge. Intentionally extremely low-poly, rough image/low-resolution textures in the spirit of Puppet Combo, but with deliberate composition, contrast and warmth. Fear and emotional interpretation depend on the viewer's imagination.

**Presentation:** existing FPS controller; environmental interactions in 3D; dialogue text integrated with the scene and small per-character typing bleeps. No NPC portraits floating over scenes, no lip sync, no voice performances. Personal photographs, PC avatars and papers may be 2D because they are physical/diegetic artifacts. No intrusive permanent gameplay HUD.

**Existing code:** preserve current bedroom, CRT computer/web/journal/agenda/save and bed->next-day systems. Time management is by meaningful engagements, NOT picking `Study +10` on a PC and sleeping. Inspect before writing or migrating Godot code.

## This micro-MVP's story scope

**3 days, dates Oct 15–17, 2003.** The player becomes familiar with June (diner staff) and Hannah (student with a film camera) and their normal speech. A natural evening conversation is remembered with the exact answer chosen by the player. Later, after Hannah leaves, text appears near the service entrance in her unmistakable conversational style; it matches previous dialogue, disintegrates into syllables/odd pacing and contains the actual selected player response labeled `???`. This should feel like a cosmically incomprehensible violation of causality or personhood, NOT an AI monster obviously rehearsing lines. The player can withdraw or investigate but sees no entity. End the experience quietly without a second reveal.

## Explicitly NOT for this micro-MVP

- No Evan-photo protagonist reveal (save for larger demo; do not spoil in player-visible content here).
- No `Second Closing`, no repeat cup/closing animation; looks like a scripting bug.
- No `The Conversation We Never Had`; a weaker variation on the same audio/text device.
- No alternate town, other-side ruin scene, double confronting Hannah or forest boss.
- No voiced dialogue, no TTS, no visible `YOU` author label for offscreen dialogue.
- No third-person gameplay, detailed facial animation rigs, animated 2D character portraits, 3 years of Tokimeki calendars.
- No crafting, guns, chasing enemies, sanity/hunger bars, romance meter, procedural missions, omniscient objective arrows.

## Mystery-writing integrity

The story authors should have private internal hypotheses, but no player-facing explanation or assertion of agency. The title *The Rehearsal* is **a development codename** only. Do not show it in game. Avoid cliches: "it knows your name", "don't trust her", red computer glitch text, VHS distortion on every object, dramatic stinger and "anomaly detected" notification. The event may have no explanation yet, and that is valid as long as its sensory cues are consistently implemented and player choice matters.
