# 07 — THE COMPUTER: DIEGETIC HOME BASE, NOT A GAMEPLAY MENU

## Existing systems are sacred until inspected

Use the user's functioning 2003-era computer: browser/search, IM, journal/agenda, save management, and bed→next-day loop. Audit current implementation; avoid reworking it for style alone. No app called `STUDY` that increments a stat and forces sleep. No menu that silently fast-forwards an unrelated activity.

## MVP usage

- **Browser:** 2–3 short, fully authored Web pages to establish 2003 small-town internet texture. Search bar recognizes limited aliases, supports link traversal/back, can display a little irrelevant content. It is intentionally **not** an LLM generating pages and not a live Internet search of 2026.
- **IM:** mundane friend message that can motivate a diner visit, another small world-building ping on Day2/Day3. No supernatural "Unknown sent you a message" cliché.
- **Journal:** brief entries after **actual** witnessed events. After The Rehearsal, a factual human entry might be: `I saw Hannah leave. A little later, the same thing she said appeared from behind the diner. And one of the lines was mine.` This is acceptable as observation, but might be too interpretive; offer a more conservative version such as `I heard the conversation again outside. I know what I chose to say.` No speculation about mimicry or time loops.
- **Calendar:** dates Wed Oct 15, Thu Oct 16, Fri Oct 17, 2003. Event availability can be visible in-world but avoid story mission checklist.
- **Save/load:** durable day, location, character memories, chosen line IDs, rehearsal progress, PC page/window state if feasible. Explicit save and a safe autosave before day rollover.

## The 2003 illusion

Classic browser styling, white backgrounds, small links/bitmaps, green URLs, 800x600-inspired layouts, odd fan sites, Web rings, forums or community notices. Do not invoke modern social apps, maps/notifications or Gmail (did not exist in 2003). Fully fictional engine/brand is safest. Internet/search pages should feel *boring enough to be real*.

## Important emotional pacing

Computer is the **refuge after the alley**, not a second horror cutscene. In the micro-MVP the return to warm bedroom should be possible without another supernatural sting. The journal is optional. Player ends the slice by voluntarily sleeping or choosing `End prototype` from a neutral PC note if no bed advance integration fits.
